Privacy Policy

This policy explains how AI Browser accesses, processes, stores, and shares information when you browse the web or choose to use its AI and content features.

Effective date: July 24, 2026

AI Browser (“AI Browser,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides a mobile web browser with optional AI assistance, news, weather, search, and related features. This Privacy Policy applies to the AI Browser Android application, this website, and support communications. It does not govern websites or services you visit through the browser.

Privacy contact: aibrowser.support@gmail.com

1. Privacy at a glance

  • Browser data such as history, bookmarks, open tabs, and downloads is generally kept on your device unless you deliberately use a feature that requires remote processing.
  • AI features are user-initiated. When you ask AI about a page, summarize it, translate it, or submit a prompt, the information needed to complete that request is transmitted to our AI service providers.
  • Incognito mode limits what AI Browser saves locally, but it does not make you anonymous to websites, search engines, network providers, employers, or schools.
  • You can clear browser data, manage permissions, and stop using optional features at any time.

3. Browser data stored on your device

AI Browser may store browsing history, bookmarks, open tabs, cookies, cached website content, downloads, autofill information, and browser preferences locally on your device. Websites may place cookies or use similar technologies under their own policies. You can clear supported categories of browsing data through browser settings or Android app settings.

We do not receive the contents of every page you visit merely because you use AI Browser. A page URL or page content may leave your device when needed for a feature you activate, when a website receives your request, when a search provider handles your query, or when security and network services are used.

4. AI features and page content

AI features do not need access to every page you visit. When you deliberately submit a prompt, choose Ask About Page, Summarize Page, Translate, or enable another page-aware AI tool, AI Browser may transmit your prompt and the page information necessary to answer it. This can include text visible on the page, selected text, the page title, the URL, and prior messages in the active conversation.

Do not use page-aware AI on banking, payment, medical, government-identification, confidential workplace, or other sensitive pages. Do not enter passwords, payment credentials, private authentication codes, or highly sensitive personal information in an AI prompt.

AI requests are processed by contracted AI infrastructure and model providers acting for the purpose of generating the requested output. We do not represent that AI responses are private communications, error-free, or suitable for professional decisions.

5. Incognito mode

When all incognito tabs are closed, AI Browser is designed not to retain their browsing history, cookies, or cached page data in the ordinary history interface. Files you download, bookmarks you create, changes made to websites, and information retained by third parties may remain. Your activity may still be visible to websites, search providers, internet or mobile network providers, employers, schools, and others that control the network or device.

6. Android permissions

AI Browser requests permissions only when needed for a feature. Depending on the features you use, these may include location for local weather, microphone for voice input, camera for visual search, notifications for alerts, and storage or media access for downloads and user-selected files. You can deny or revoke permissions in Android settings. Some features will not work without their corresponding permission.

7. How we use information

  • Provide requested browser, search, AI, news, weather, download, and personalization functions.
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our Terms of Use, and protect users.
  • Diagnose crashes, monitor performance, and improve product reliability and usability.
  • Display and measure advertising and apply your consent and privacy choices.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and legal requests.
  • Meet legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

8. When information is shared

We may disclose information only as described below:

  • Service providers: hosting, AI inference, search, weather and news delivery, analytics, crash reporting, security, advertising, and customer-support providers process information to deliver their contracted services.
  • Websites and search providers: when you visit a website or submit a search, that third party receives information ordinarily included in web requests, such as your IP address, browser information, cookies, and the URL or query requested.
  • Legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate abuse, or respond to lawful process.
  • Business transfers: information may be transferred in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

We do not sell the contents of your browsing history or AI conversations for money. Advertising and analytics providers may process identifiers and activity data as described above. Where applicable law treats certain advertising disclosures as a “sale” or “sharing,” we will provide legally required choices.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encrypted network transmission where supported. No browser, website, storage system, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your device, browser, and accounts secure.

11. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information; object to or restrict certain processing; withdraw consent; or appeal a denied request. You may also have the right to opt out of certain targeted advertising, sale, or sharing of personal information.

To exercise a right, contact aibrowser.support@gmail.com. We will not discriminate against you for making a privacy request. You may also control browser data in the app, Android permissions in device settings, and advertising choices through Android and any consent controls presented in the app.

12. Legal bases for EEA, UK, and Swiss users

Where applicable, we process information because it is necessary to provide a feature you requested, because you consented, because we have a legitimate interest in securing and improving the service that is not overridden by your rights, or because processing is required by law. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.

13. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than where you live. Where required, we use recognized safeguards for cross-border transfers. Privacy and data-protection laws may differ between countries.

14. Children

AI Browser is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If local law requires a higher minimum age, that higher age applies. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided personal information should contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required.

15. Third-party websites and services

AI Browser can access third-party websites, search engines, news, weather, advertisements, and other services. Their privacy practices are controlled by them, not by us. Review their policies before providing personal information.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect product, legal, or operational changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when required. Material changes apply prospectively unless law permits otherwise.
